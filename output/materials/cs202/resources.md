# Further Learning and Resources

To deepen your understanding of tokenization and experiment with different tokenizers, explore the following resources:

*   **Tiktokenizer (OpenAI):** A tool to see how OpenAI's models tokenize text.
    [https://tiktokenizer.vercel.app/](https://tiktokenizer.vercel.app/)

*   **Hugging Face Tokenizer Playground:** An interactive playground to understand how various tokenizers work.
    [https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/tokenizer_summary](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/tokenizer_summary)

*   **Andrej Karpathy -- "Let's Build the GPT Tokenizer"**: A comprehensive 2-hour 15-minute deep dive tutorial on building a tokenizer from scratch.
    (Search for this video on YouTube for the full tutorial)
