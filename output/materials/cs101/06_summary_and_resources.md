# Summary and Resources

Tokenization is not just a preprocessing step; it fundamentally shapes how Large Language Models perceive and interact with human language. By converting raw text into meaningful subword units, tokenizers enable LLMs to manage vast vocabularies, handle unseen words, and efficiently process information in a numerical format.

Understanding tokenization, especially algorithms like BPE, is key to comprehending the strengths and limitations of modern LLMs. It directly impacts a model's performance, speed, and ability to generalize across diverse texts.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Fundamental First Step:** LLMs operate on tokens, not raw text.
*   **Numerical Conversion:** Tokens allow text to be represented as numbers for computational processing.
