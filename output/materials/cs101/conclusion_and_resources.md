# Conclusion and Further Resources

Tokenization is a foundational and often overlooked aspect of working with Large Language Models. By understanding how raw text is transformed into numerical tokens, you gain deeper insight into an LLM's capabilities and limitations. Subword tokenization methods like BPE are key to managing vocabulary efficiently and handling the vast diversity of human language.

## Explore Tokenization in Practice

To further your understanding and experiment with tokenization, consider exploring these resources:

*   **Tiktokenizer (OpenAI):** A fantastic web tool to see how different text inputs are tokenized by OpenAI's models (e.g., GPT-3, GPT-4). You can input text and visualize the tokens and their IDs.
    *   Link: https://tiktokenizer.vercel.app/

*   **Hugging Face Tokenizer Playground:** The Hugging Face `transformers` library is a cornerstone for working with LLMs. Their tokenizer summary and playground offer interactive ways to understand various tokenizer types and their behavior.
    *   Link: https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/tokenizer_summary

## Deep Dive Tutorial

For those who want a comprehensive, hands-on understanding of how to build a tokenizer from scratch, Andrej Karpathy's tutorial is highly recommended:

*   **Andrej Karpathy -- "Let's Build the GPT Tokenizer"** (approximately 2 hours 15 minutes deep dive)
    *   This tutorial walks you through the implementation details, offering invaluable insights into the practical aspects of tokenizer design and development.
