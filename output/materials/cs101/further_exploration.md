# Further Exploration and Resources

To deepen your understanding of tokenization and experiment with different tokenizers, explore the following resources:

## Online Tools and Playgrounds

*   **Tiktokenizer (OpenAI):** This interactive tool allows you to see how text is tokenized by OpenAI's models (like GPT-3, GPT-4). It's an excellent way to get hands-on experience and visualize tokens.
    *   Link: `https://tiktokenizer.vercel.app/`

*   **Hugging Face Tokenizer Playground:** The Hugging Face ecosystem provides a wealth of tools for NLP. Their tokenizer summary and playground offer insights into various tokenizer types used by different transformer models.
    *   Link: `https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/tokenizer_summary`

## Deep Dive Tutorial

*   **Andrej Karpathy -- "Let's Build the GPT Tokenizer"**: For those who want a comprehensive, code-level understanding of how a modern tokenizer is built from scratch, this 2-hour and 15-minute deep dive by Andrej Karpathy is highly recommended.
